# Licensing, branding and asset policy
Status: owner decision required. Repository is public but no project license has been approved; public visibility does not grant broad redistribution rights. Recommend evaluating GPL-3.0-or-later for tightly coupled shell components (especially if derived from GPL upstream) and MIT/Apache-2.0 for independent components only after compatibility review. Do not add a blanket license without owner approval and dependency analysis.

Actual macOS is the sole visual reference. Official Apple images in docs/design/references.md are linked to publisher-hosted files, not copied into releases. Use original iconography, wallpapers, fonts with redistribution rights, and Jadro branding. No Apple/Microsoft trademarks as project identity or implication of endorsement. No proprietary OS binaries, frameworks, recovery images, paid apps or fonts in repo. Review trademarks/trade dress and applicable rights before release; this is engineering guidance, not legal advice.

Maintain component SPDX identifiers after license decision, third-party notices, source obligations, license inventory and SBOM. Check Qt/KDE licensing and distribution obligations, NVIDIA/Steam terms, Flatpak remotes, models, provider SDK terms and Windows licensing. Do not redistribute proprietary installers without permission. All third-party asset additions require source, license, attribution and review.
